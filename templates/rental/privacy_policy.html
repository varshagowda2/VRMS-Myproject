Privacy Policy

Vehicle Rental Management System (VRMS)

This Privacy Policy explains how VRMS collects, uses, and protects information provided while using this educational project.

1. Information We Collect

The system may store customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, vehicle rental details, reservation dates, and payment records entered into the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to manage customer records, vehicles, reservations, invoices, and payments within the rental management system.

3. Data Security

Access to personal information should be restricted to authorised users. Strong passwords, secure hosting, HTTPS, and appropriate database permissions should be used before real customer information is stored.

4. Data Sharing

Personal information should not be shared except where necessary for operating the service or where legally required.

5. Data Retention and Requests

Information should be retained only as necessary for legitimate operational and legal purposes. Users should be provided with an appropriate contact method for privacy-related requests.

6. Applicable Privacy Laws

Where applicable, the system operator must assess and comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and rules in force, and the GDPR where it applies. The required notices, consent, rights, retention practices, and security measures depend on the actual use of the system and applicable law.

7. Contact

For this educational project, contact details for the responsible administrator should be added before public deployment.
